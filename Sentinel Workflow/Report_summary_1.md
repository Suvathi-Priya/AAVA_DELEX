### B. Quality Metrics Formulas

**Calculation Methods (Ready for Application):**

- **Coverage Percentage** = (Covered Acceptance Criteria / Total Acceptance Criteria) × 100
- **Defect Rate** = (Total Defects / Total Test Cases) × 100
- **Defect Severity Rate** = ((Critical Defects + High Severity Defects) / Total Defects) × 100
- **Execution Success Rate** = (Total Passed / Total Executed) × 100
- **Test Execution Rate** = (Total Executed / Total Test Cases) × 100
